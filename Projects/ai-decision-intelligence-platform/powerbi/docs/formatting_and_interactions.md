# Formatting and interactions

- Canvas: 1280×720, `#F8FAFC`; visual background white; 6 px corner radius.
- Primary blue `#2563EB`, teal `#14B8A6`, warning amber `#F59E0B`, critical red
  `#DC2626`, neutral slate `#64748B`.
- Titles: Segoe UI Semibold 12 pt; page title 20 pt; cards 24 pt; body 10–11 pt.
- Risk colors: Critical red, At Risk amber, Healthy green, Excess blue, Obsolete Risk purple.
- Use zero baselines for quantity/cost bars. Use diverging axes for bias and scenario impacts.
- Currency: `$#,0` on cards, `$#,0.00` in details. Percentages: `0.0%`.
- Use conditional icons for solver status and quality status; retain text for accessibility.
- Slicers affect all visuals on their page except fixed baseline comparison cards.
- Sync Date, Product, Category, Store, Warehouse, and Scenario slicers only where meaningful.
- Add alt text to every visual and maintain at least 4.5:1 text contrast.
- Label generated operational data as synthetic in the report information panel.
